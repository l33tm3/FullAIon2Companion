aion2.th.gl creature portraits (thgl-icons: assets/thgl, the Atlas map in the panel)
https://aion2.th.gl/es/db/bestiary

45 family portraits for creatures the game client ships without one (Durgal,
Tuka, Frugon, Ghula...), taken from aion2.th.gl's database with its authors'
permission, on condition that they are credited on the Daeva Companion website.
The Atlas credits them too. The game artwork belongs to NCSOFT.

aion2.exchange (Aion2Market) Market data (the Mercado tab and the loot HUD)
https://aion2.exchange

The prices, listings and 24 h changes of the game's Market on each server come
from aion2.exchange, which records them every hour. They are used with its
authors' permission and credited in the Mercado tab ("Datos: aion2.exchange").
The app only reads them (one request per server every 30 minutes at most, plus
its server list once a day) and sends nothing about the player.
